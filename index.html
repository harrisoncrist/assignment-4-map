The Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) provides data on property purchases. This map shows the average loan values for each census tract in New York City for 2018 and 2023. Mortgage loans can be used to explore how values moves throughout the geography of a city. Referred to cataclysmics money, or money of various forms, you can see where value is pooling, creating margins and peripheries that can show what areas are up and down. Even more important, than absolute values, is the relative change in values from year to year. This type of data collects mortage data and puts it into action.
