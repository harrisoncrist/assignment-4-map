How does value move through New York City? The answer to that question has consequences for the entire urban fabric and the people who live wihtin it. We have plenty of concepts to think about it. Jane Jacobs writes about it in terms of cataclysmic money. David Harvey presents how it is wrapped up with the creation and destruction of a public commons. Merriman describes it in terms of value flows being the very stuff of shaping the urban political economy. Thanks to these great urban thinkers and activists, we have several ways to conceptualize how value moves through the city and an appreciation of its consequences. We seem to be lacking a good way to measure and visualize these dynamics. The Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) provides one way to make inroads on this questtion. The HDMA provides detailed data on mortgages, down to the census tract level, giving us access to a wealth of data. Two points stand out as particularly useful for the question of how value moves through space - which is mortgage values.
