This project leverages the Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) dataset to visualize average home purchase loan values in New York City by census tract for 2018 and 2023, and the percent change in average home purchase loan values between the two years.

Click through Map Options or read below to explore the map

Urban theorists and academics have shown the importance of understanding the movement of financial value in and around our cities. If there is too little value in a neighborhood, it suffers from disinvestment and marginalization [1]. On the other hand, too much financial value can destroy cohesive neghborhoods [2], accelerate displacement [3], and exploit collective social resources [4].

Given these stakes, it is critical to develop ways to visualize the movement of financial capital.
