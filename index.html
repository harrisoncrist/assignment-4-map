Mortgage Loans in New York City

Home Data Methods

Urban theorists and academics have shown the importance of understanding the movement of financial value in and around our cities. If there is too little value pooled in a neighborhood, it suffers from disinvestment and marginalization. On the other hand, too much financial value can undermine stability, accelerate displacement, and exploit collective social resources.

Given these stakes, it is critical to develop ways to visualize the movement of financial capital.

This project takes a step in this direction by leveraging the Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) dataset, a rich source of mortgage lending data. The maps on the right visualize average home purchase loan values by census tract for 2018 and 2023, and the percent change in average home purchase loan values between the two years.
